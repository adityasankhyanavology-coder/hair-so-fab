WEEKLY CO-WASH ROUTINE

Co-wash at least 3 times per week to keep curls soft and manageable.

PREP HAIR

Divide hair into 4 sections and secure with clips.

WET THOROUGHLY

Completely wet all sections to avoid tangling.

APPLY CONDITIONER

Use a Denman brush. Start detangling from ends and work up..

DEEP CONDITION

Allow the conditioner to sit for 15-30 minutes.sit for 15-30 minutes..

RINSE HAIR

Rinse your hair thoroughly with cool water.

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE FOR DEFINING YOUR CURLS

Follow these steps after co-wash for the best curl definition.

1

DETANGLE

Divide hair into 3–4 sections and detangle thoroughly using a Denman brush.

2

SPRAY MIX

Mix 2/3 water and 1/3 conditioner in a spray bottle. Spray each section generously.

3

SEAL MOISTURE

Apply a light sealant cream evenly on each section where the product is applied.

4

SCRUNCH

After washing, use a cotton cloth to scrunch each section to form curls.

5

DIFFUSE DRY

Use a blow dryer with diffuser until hair is at least 60% dry. Air-drying is also an option.

6

REFRESH

Between co-washes, use water/conditioner mix spray to refresh your curls.

WAVY AND STRAIGHT HAIR CARE

Co-washing, or conditioner-only washing, is a gentle way to keep your extensions soft, manageable, and tangle-free. Follow these steps to co-wash your straight hair extensions effectively.

1

PREPARING YOUR EXTENSIONS
Section Your Hair:

Divide your hair into manageable sections using alligator clips. This helps to ensure even application of the conditioner and prevents tangling.

Wet Your Hair:

Use cool water to thoroughly wet each section of your hair. This will help the conditioner spread more easily and effectively.

2

APPLYING THE CONDITIONER
Apply Conditioner::

Choose a moisturizing conditioner and apply a generous amount to each section. Use your fingers or a wide-tooth comb to distribute the conditioner evenly from the mid-lengths to the ends of the extensions.

Deep Condition:

Leave the conditioner on for 15-30 minutes. This allows the hair to absorb the moisture and nutrients, keeping it soft and supple.

3

RINSING & DETANGLING
Rinse:

Rinse your hair thoroughly with cool water. Cool water helps seal the cuticles, locking in moisture and enhancing the shine of your extensions..

Detangle:

While rinsing, gently detangle your hair using a wide-tooth comb or your fingers. Start from the ends and work your way up to prevent breakage.

4

DRYING & STYLING
Remove Excess Water:

Gently squeeze out excess water using a cotton cloth. Avoid rubbing the hair, as this can cause frizz and tangling.

Air Dry:

Allow your extensions to air dry naturally. If necessary, use a blow dryer on a low heat setting to speed up the process. Always use heat protectant.

Style as Desired:

Once dry, style your straight hair extensions as usual. Avoid excessive heat styling to maintain their integrity and longevity.

By following these steps, your hair extensions will remain sleek, smooth, and in pristine condition. Regular co-washing will ensure your extensions look and feel their best!
